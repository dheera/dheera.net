## A landscape measured in heat

The colors in this series represent thermal measurements. They reveal contrasts that an ordinary photograph cannot show, turning a familiar landscape into a different kind of map.

The original project combines a regular black-and-white photograph with data captured by a thermal camera. A custom rig scans the scene to build a wider, more detailed view.
