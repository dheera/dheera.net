I'm Dheera Venkatraman — a software and AI engineer, photographer, and world traveller based in the San Francisco Bay Area. I like understanding how things work, making things that don't exist yet, and finding a different way to look at something familiar.

## Engineering and imagination

My work spans generative AI, computer vision, robotics, and imaging. I'm a Principal Engineer at Wayve, where I work on generative models for evaluating autonomous driving. Previously, I worked at Amazon and Freedom Robotics, and co-founded Robby Technologies, building autonomous delivery robots. I have a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from MIT, where my research included single-photon imaging.

That technical background is also part of how I make art. A camera can be an instrument for seeing beyond ordinary perception: the heat of an Icelandic landscape, or the scale of a nebula above a mountain. I enjoy the space where a technical experiment becomes an image, an experience, or something useful.

## Across places and cultures

I've never had a simple answer to “Where are you from?” I'm a third-culture kid; language, culture, nationality, and belonging don't line up neatly for me. I feel at home moving between places and perspectives.

Travel and photography give that curiosity somewhere to go. I like paying attention to light, landscapes, and the details of everyday life — and bringing some of those observations back into the things I build.

## Things made out of curiosity

Most of my professional engineering happens inside companies. This site is a home for the work I can share: photographs, open-source tools, physical builds, and occasional experiments on the web.
